Lesson 1 — What Is Computer Networking?

Definition: Computer networking is the connection of two or more devices so they can exchange data and share resources using agreed communication protocols.

Purpose

  • Share files, printers, and internet access.
  • Communicate between computers, servers, and mobile devices.
  • Transfer data securely and reliably.

Real-world example

When you open a website on your laptop, your device sends network traffic through your local network and router toward the website's server. The server sends data back to your device.

Essential networking terms

  • Host: A device that sends or receives network data.
  • IP address: A logical address used to identify an interface on an IP network.
  • MAC address: A link-layer address used for communication on many local networks.
  • Router: Forwards packets between IP networks.
  • Switch: Forwards Ethernet frames within a local network.
  • Protocol: Rules that devices follow to communicate.
  • Packet: A unit of data carried by a network-layer protocol.

Exam question

Explain computer networking in your own words and distinguish between a router and a switch.
